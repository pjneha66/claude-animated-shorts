The Bridge and the Borrowed Step.

Mara waited beside the iron bridge in Broughton in 1831. Elise stood by the river with a wooden basket.
Tomas marched toward them with the soldiers. Mara pointed at the bridge and looked at Elise.
Elise reached toward Mara and held both hands together. Tomas looked back at them before he stepped onto the deck.
Mara walked alongside Elise beneath the warm afternoon sky. Tomas marched in step as the iron supports began to sway.
Elise noticed a loose bolt and pointed toward Tomas. Mara reached toward Elise as the bridge suddenly cracked.
Tomas ran toward the bank and waved both arms. Elise held Mara as the soldiers broke step and rushed to safety.
Mara stood beside Tomas and looked at the empty crossing. Elise held both their hands as dust drifted across the river.
Tomas pointed toward the long road around the water. Mara smiled at Elise and walked with both of them toward the sunset.
