The Last Light at Grey Point.

At midnight in November, a fog rolled into the harbour of Grey Point. The keeper, Mara, climbed the iron stairs with a lantern. The lamp was dark. In the glass, a second lantern glowed where no one stood.

Mara looked out at the sea. A boat drifted toward the rocks, silent and empty. She raised the lantern and pointed toward the channel. The fog thickened. The second light stepped closer.

A shadow crossed the stairwell. Mara turned, and the lantern went out. The boat changed course. By dawn, the lamp was lit again, and the rocks were empty. The harbour log recorded no vessel that night.
