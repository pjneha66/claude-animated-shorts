A Promise in the Garden.

On a warm spring morning, Elise waited by the old stone bench with a ribbon in her hand. Tomas arrived late, carrying a small paper parcel. He pointed at the blossom tree, where two birds had built a nest.

They sat together. Elise laughed at the ribbon tangled around her fingers. Tomas opened the parcel: a sketch of the garden, drawn the winter before. She looked at him, then at the tree, and smiled.

The wind lifted the ribbon into the branches. Tomas reached for it. Elise held out her hand instead. By sunset the sketch lay on the bench, and the ribbon still hung in the tree.
